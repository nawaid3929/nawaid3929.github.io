About

I turn messy business processes into clean, secure SharePoint solutions: portals people open every morning, document libraries with real approval chains, and automations that remove the manual follow-up.

I've worked on both SharePoint Online and on-premises (2013 and 2016), for clients ranging from manufacturing and mining to diagnostics and aviation. My day-to-day stack is SPFx with React, REST APIs, and the Power Platform.
